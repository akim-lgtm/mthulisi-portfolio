Grabb

Buy and sell, without the friction.

Grabb is a marketplace for everyday people. List the things you no longer need, find what you're actually looking for, and deal directly with the person on the other side. It's built for the kind of buying and selling that happens between neighbours — not warehouses.

Mthulisi is the founder behind Grabb, shaping the idea and the way people will use it while he studies software development.
